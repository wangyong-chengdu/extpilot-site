LeadSnap — Email & Phone Extractor

Extract emails, phone numbers, and social media links from any webpage in 30 seconds. Export to CSV or Excel. 100% local — no data leaves your browser.

Install for Chrome Install for Edge

LeadSnap is a Chrome and Edge extension that extracts emails, phone numbers, and social media profile links from any public webpage in under 30 seconds. Click the extension icon on any page, and LeadSnap scans the visible content to find contact information — then displays results organized by type (emails, phones, socials) with one-click copy and CSV/TXT export.

Unlike tools that only extract emails, LeadSnap captures all three contact types in a single scan. Phone numbers are parsed with international format support (via libphonenumber), and social links cover LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and GitHub. The free plan includes unlimited single-page scans, 50 URLs per day in batch AutoVisit mode, 100 local history records, and CSV/TXT export — with no sign-up required.

All data processing happens locally in your browser. LeadSnap uses only 3 core permissions (activeTab, storage, clipboardWrite) — fewer than any competing tool. Extracted contacts are stored in browser-local storage and never sent to any external server. Pro ($3.99/month) unlocks 1,000 AutoVisit URLs per day, unlimited history, XLSX export, and clean export footers without branding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does LeadSnap extract from a webpage?

LeadSnap extracts three types of contact information: email addresses, phone numbers (with international format support), and social media profile links (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, GitHub). Results appear in the popup within 2 seconds.

Is LeadSnap free to use?

Yes. The free plan includes unlimited single-page scans, up to 50 AutoVisit URLs per day, 100 local history records, and CSV/TXT export. No sign-up or credit card required.

Does LeadSnap send my data to a server?

No. All extraction and storage happens locally in your browser using chrome.storage and IndexedDB. The only network request is an optional license key check for Pro users.

How is LeadSnap different from other email extractors?

Most email extractor extensions only find emails. LeadSnap extracts emails, phones, and social links in one scan. It uses fewer permissions (3 vs 5-10), costs 1/5 the price of comparable tools, and produces clean exports without embedded ads.

What is AutoVisit mode?

AutoVisit lets you paste a list of URLs and LeadSnap visits each one automatically, extracting contacts and compiling all results into a single export file. Free users can process 50 URLs/day; Pro users get 1,000 URLs/day.
