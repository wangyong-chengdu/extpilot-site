What to include

Common questions

License key not working? Check that you entered it in the extension's upgrade dialog exactly as received (dashes included). If it still fails, email us with your purchase receipt.

Where is my data? All our extensions process your files locally in the browser. See each product's privacy page (linked on its product page) for details.

Refunds? For Pro purchases via Stripe, email us within 14 days with your receipt and we'll process a refund.
