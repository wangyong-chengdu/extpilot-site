Pro unlocks unlimited Groq transcription, unlimited history, and AI summaries with your own AI API key.

FAQ

Does tab recording mute my computer?

No. The extension reconnects captured tab audio to your output so you can keep listening while transcribing.

Can I edit transcript text?

Yes. Once recording or upload transcription is done, the transcript box becomes editable before copy, export, or summary.

Do I need a Groq key?

Yes for Groq transcription. Add your Groq transcription key in Settings. Web Speech can be used without a Groq key.

Where do I activate Pro?

Open Settings, use Upgrade & Activate Pro, buy a license, paste the license key, and click Activate.
